# Frontend & UI Glossary: ARISTURTLE Edition

This glossary helps translate visual ideas into technical instructions. Use these terms to describe exactly how you want a section to look, feel, and move.

---

## 1. Layout & Structure
*Where things sit on the page.*

*   **Hero Section:** The very first thing a user sees (usually the big video/image at the top).
*   **Viewport (VH/VW):** The visible area of the browser window. (e.g., "Make this section 100vh" means "Make it fill the entire screen height").
*   **Grid System:** An invisible 12-column structure used to align elements.
*   **Flexbox:** A way to align items in a row or column (e.g., "Center these three cards horizontally").
*   **Container:** The maximum width of the content (usually centered with empty space on the sides).
*   **Asymmetry:** Intentionally "unbalanced" layouts where elements are staggered to create a sense of motion.
*   **Z-Index:** The "stacking order." High Z-index elements sit "on top" of others (like a sticky navigation bar).

---

## 2. Spacing & Sizing
*The "breathability" of the design.*

*   **Padding:** Space *inside* an element (between the border and the content).
*   **Margin:** Space *outside* an element (between two different sections/cards).
*   **Aspect Ratio:** The proportional relationship between width and height (e.g., 16:9 for the Hero video).
*   **Negative Space:** The empty areas between elements that prevent the site from feeling cluttered.

---

## 3. Typography & Text
*The "Voice" of the brand.*

*   **Display:** Huge, high-impact text (used for the Hero headline).
*   **Headline:** Section titles (H2, H3).
*   **Body:** Standard paragraph text.
*   **Label/Caption:** Small, often all-caps technical data or tags.
*   **Kerning/Tracking:** The space between individual letters.
*   **Leading:** The space between lines of text (line-height).

---

## 4. Components & Surfaces
*The "Parts" of the car.*

*   **CTA (Call to Action):** A button or link designed to get a click (e.g., "Join the Grid").
*   **Card:** A self-contained box with an image, title, and text (e.g., the "Custom Motors" block).
*   **Surface:** The background layer an element sits on.
*   **Glassmorphism:** A semi-transparent background with a blur effect (like our navigation bar).
*   **Overlay:** A layer that sits on top of an image to make text readable (like a dark gradient over a video).
*   **Tooltip:** A small text box that appears when you hover over something.

---

## 5. Motion & Interaction
*How the site "feels" when used.*

*   **Hover State:** What happens when the mouse moves over an element (e.g., "The image should scale up 5%").
*   **Scroll Reveal:** When an element fades or slides into view as you scroll down.
*   **Staggered Animation:** When multiple items (like a grid of 3 cards) appear one after another with a slight delay.
*   **Parallax:** When the background moves slower than the foreground, creating a 3D depth effect.
*   **Easing:** The "speed curve" of an animation (e.g., "Ease-in-out" feels smooth; "Linear" feels mechanical).
*   **Keyframes:** Specific "checkpoints" in an animation (e.g., "At 50%, turn the border orange").

---

## 6. Technical Accents (The "Aristurtle" Specials)
*Specific to our Engineering aesthetic.*

*   **Telemetry Overlay:** Technical lines, grids, or numbers that appear over images.
*   **Scan-line:** A subtle moving horizontal line that mimics a computer screen or sensor scan.
*   **Tonal Shift:** Using different shades of dark gray (rather than lines) to separate sections.
*   **Blueprint Effect:** A high-contrast, line-art style for images.

---

### Example Instruction using this Glossary:
> "In the **Hero Section**, use a **100vh** height. Apply a **Dark Overlay** with a **Z-Index** lower than the **Display Typography**. Ensure the **CTA** has a **Hover State** that increases **Tracking** and changes the **Surface** color to **Secondary Orange**."
