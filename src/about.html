About Aristurtle

We are the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki Racing Team Electric & Autonomous. Since 2013, we've been pushing the boundaries of Greek engineering on the world stage of Formula Student.

Our Story

The journey from a small workshop in Thessaloniki to the top of European racing podiums. A decade of mechanical evolution.

2013 — PRESENT
2013

ELECTRA

The spark that started it all. Our first-ever electric race car, laying the foundation for high-voltage systems and weight-saving strategies in Greek academia.

2018

THETIS

The introduction of Carbon Fiber monocoque technology. Thetis brought us closer to the elite tier of FS competitions with unprecedented structural rigidity.

2024

CALYPSO

Our current pinnacle. A dual-motor, four-wheel drive masterpiece featuring integrated autonomous sensor suites and advanced torque vectoring algorithms.

Mission

To cultivate the next generation of Greek Engineers through the relentless pursuit of performance and technological disruption.

Vision

Becoming the global benchmark for autonomous electric racing systems and sustainable motorsport innovation.
